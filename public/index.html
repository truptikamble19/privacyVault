KNOWLEDGE IS CONTROL

Privacy guide.

Practical ways to understand and manage your digital footprint.

01

Understand your data

Platforms may process account details, prompts, uploaded files, usage information and preferences. The categories and retention periods vary by service and settings.

02

Review privacy settings

Look for conversation history, training preferences, activity controls, export tools and account deletion options in each service's official settings.

03

Know your data rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and the platform, you may be able to request access, correction or erasure. Eligibility, exceptions and response times vary.

04

Keep a clear record

Record the date, request type, confirmation and response. Keep reference numbers and evidence so you can follow up if needed.

05

Protect sensitive information

Do not share passwords, OTPs, payment credentials, private documents or other sensitive personal information unnecessarily in prompts.

06

Understand this dashboard

This project is a privacy-management interface. Its demo risk scores and sample data are illustrative, not a security audit or verified account scan.
